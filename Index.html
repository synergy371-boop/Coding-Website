Real-Time Material Tracking

Material delays are one of the biggest reasons EPC projects slip. With real-time visibility into every shipment—where it is, when it will arrive, and whether it passed quality checks— project teams can cut down on firefighting and keep schedules, costs, and productivity under control.

Explore the Solution

The Problem

EPC projects depend on thousands of materials moving across vendors, warehouses, ports, and sites. When a critical item is delayed or lost in transit, entire work fronts stall. Today, most teams rely on phone calls, emails, and spreadsheets to track materials—leading to blind spots, surprises, and reactive decision-making.

Our Idea: Real-Time Material Tracking

The idea is simple: connect every material movement to a single live view. Using IoT tags, logistics integrations, and smart alerts, project teams can see the status of each shipment in real time and act before delays hit the schedule.

Key Capabilities

Live Location Tracking

Track materials from vendor to site with GPS or IoT tags, so you always know where critical items are and how far they are from the project.

Predictive Arrival Times

Use traffic, port congestion, and carrier performance data to estimate realistic ETAs instead of relying on static dates in spreadsheets.

Quality Status at a Glance

Link inspection and test reports to each shipment, so teams can see whether materials have passed quality checks before they reach the site.

Early-Warning Alerts

Automatically flag high-risk shipments—late dispatch, route deviations, failed inspections—so planners can adjust work fronts and avoid idle time.

Impact on EPC Projects

Fewer Schedule Slips

With better visibility, planners can resequence work, reorder materials, or switch vendors before delays become critical path issues.

Lower Firefighting

Site teams spend less time chasing information and more time executing planned work, reducing stress and last-minute chaos.

Improved Productivity

Crews are less likely to be idle waiting for materials, which directly improves labor productivity and project margins.

Real-Time Material Tracking is about turning uncertainty into clarity—so EPC projects can move from reactive firefighting to confident, data-driven execution.
